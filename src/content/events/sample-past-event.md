**This is a sample.** It shows how an event looks after its date has passed. It is not a real event, and it did not happen.

Once an event is over, the site moves it from "Upcoming events" to "Past events" by itself. No one has to edit the file. The post can then be updated with a short write-up of what happened, in plain words and only with facts the foundation can stand behind.

Photos can be added in a `gallery` list in the file. Every photo needs a short description, and photos of children need written consent from a parent or guardian first. See `HOW-TO-ADD-POSTS.md`.
